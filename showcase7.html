Our Patented Workflow

1. Hash in Air-Gapped Room: Documents are transformed into secure hash codes offline for absolute confidentiality.

2. Upload to Blockchain: Hash codes and credentials are uploaded to the immutable blockchain.

3. Issue Secure PDFs: Students receive PDFs embedded with their unique digital footprint.

4. Instant Verification: Anyone can upload the PDF to our site to verify authenticity in seconds.
